# Research Design

## Research Question

Can long-only equity factor indices in developed markets be combined to provide more robust risk-adjusted performance than simple allocation rules and the MSCI World benchmark in a historical walk-forward test?

## Scope

The analysis focuses on developed equity markets and the following long-only indices:

- Value
- Momentum
- Quality
- Minimum Volatility
- Equal Weighted as an approximate size tilt

The MSCI World Index is used as the broad market benchmark and is not treated as a factor sleeve.

## Research Objective

The objective is not to identify the historically best allocation. It is to test whether the results remain stable across estimation windows, rebalancing frequencies and portfolio constraints.

Portfolio weights are estimated from past observations and applied to the following period. This is a historical walk-forward analysis. Since the full dataset is already available during the research process, it should not be interpreted as a genuinely untouched out-of-sample test.

## Research Stages

1. Data preparation
2. Exploratory factor analysis
3. Static portfolio construction
4. Rolling portfolio optimization
5. Historical walk-forward evaluation
6. Robustness analysis
