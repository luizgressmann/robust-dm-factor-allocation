# Research Design

## Research Question

Can evidence-based equity factors in developed markets be combined to gain better out-of-sample risk-adjusted  perfromance relative to simple allocation rules and a broad market benachmark?

## Scope

The analysis focuses on developed equity markets and the following long-only factors:

- Value
- Momentum
- Quality
- Minimum Volatility
- Equal Weighted as a size proxy

Also, the MSCI World Index is used as a broad market benchmark.

## Research Objective

The objective is not to identify the historical best-performing allocation of facots. Instead, the project aims to evaluate whether facot allocations remain robust across different estimation windows, rebalancing frequencies and portfolio constraints.

## Research Stages

1. Data preparation
2. Exploratory factor analysis
3. Static protfolio construction
4. Rolling portfolio optimization
5. Out-of-sample evaluation
6. Robustness analysis
